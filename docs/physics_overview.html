PrismaticCell · model reference

Physics of the distributed electro-thermal cell model

A prismatic LFP cell as a network of coupled equivalent circuits inside a 3-D thermal solid — every control volume carries its own electrochemical state, and heat and electricity feed back on each other every step.

LFP / graphite · one ECM per control volume · energy-conserving to ~10⁻¹²

1THERMAL

Geometry & homogenization

The cell is a fixed metal can holding two jellyrolls; each jellyroll is a stack of five-layer sandwiches — cathode collector (Al) | cathode (LFP) | separator | anode (graphite) | anode collector (Cu). The domain is a structured control-volume grid; each active CV is a homogenized piece of the sandwich stack with anisotropic conductivity: series (harmonic) through the layers, parallel (thickness-weighted) along the foils.

k⊥ = Σ tiΣ ti/ki     k∥ = Σ tikiΣ ti     (ρcp)eff = Σ tiρiciΣ ti (1)

Through-plane conductivity lands ~20–60× below in-plane — the physical reason heat leaves a prismatic cell far more easily along the foils than across the stack. All properties come from the material database; nothing is baked into solver code.

2ELECTRICAL

One equivalent circuit per control volume

Every active CV runs its own Thévenin model at its local state of charge and local temperature: open-circuit voltage, an ohmic resistance, and RC pairs for charge-transfer/diffusion dynamics.

vk = Uocv(zk) − jkR0(zk,Tk) − Σp up,k (2)

All parameters are SOC-dependent lookup tables with Arrhenius temperature scaling — this is one leg of the two-way coupling:

3ELECTRICAL

Current-collector network — why tab placement matters

The CVs are not independent: they are wired through the Al and Cu foil potential fields. Each foil obeys in-plane sheet-charge conservation, sourced by the local through-cell current and terminated at the tab weld footprints:

∇·(σt ∇φ±) = ∓ j·Acv (5)
φ+k − φ−k = vk      Σk ik = Iapplied (6)

Local closure (6) couples the foils to every ECM; the terminal constraint fixes the total. Tabs enter as physical conductances Gtab = σ nfoilsw t/L distributed over the weld footprint, so tab position, size, and protrusion genuinely redistribute current, SOC, and heat. Two fidelities: planar (one shared foil potential per roll, 2.5-D) and layered (a foil potential per through-thickness layer — full 3-D collector).

4COUPLED

Heat generation — the electrical→thermal leg

Each CV deposits Bernardi heat: irreversible overpotential heat plus reversible entropic heat (sign-changing for LFP, from the measured dU/dT table):

qk = ik(Uocv − vk) − ikTk dUdT (7)

Two more sources close the electrical ledger exactly:

qfoil = σt |∇φ|2      Ptab = Σnodes Gn(φn − Vterm)2 (8)

The tab is a 1-D fin with uniform self-heating: half of Ptab returns through the weld into the cell (all of it when the terminal is not heat-sunk), the rest exits to the terminal. Its axial profile is analytic:

T(ξ) = Troot + (Tsink − Troot)ξ + Ptab2G ξ(1−ξ) (9)

The terminal boundary condition flips the near-tab signature: a coolant-sunk busbar makes the welds the coldest spots on the foil; a floating terminal makes them the hottest. Configurable as heat-sunk at coolant, heat-sunk at an explicit busbar temperature, or adiabatic.

5THERMAL

3-D anisotropic thermal solver

ρcp ∂T∂t = ∇·(k ∇T) + q‴ (10)

Finite-volume on the structured grid; face conductances are harmonic means of the two cells' directional conductivities (exact for series composites). Time integration is backward Euler — unconditionally stable, with the factorization cached and reused across steps:

(M/Δt + A) Tn+1 = (M/Δt) Tn + bbc + q‴V (11)

7COUPLED

Two-way co-simulation loop

Per timestep, iterated to convergence (the thermal→electrical leg closes the loop):

  • Solve the collector network: foil potentials, per-CV currents, terminal voltage — using each CV's parameters at its current local temperature.
  • Form the heat map: Bernardi + foil ohmic + tab Joule backflow.
  • Advance the 3-D temperature field (backward Euler).
  • Re-evaluate R0, Rp, Cp at the new temperatures; sub-iterate until ‖ΔT‖ < tolerance.
  • Advance SOC and RC states with the converged currents.

Hot regions get lower resistance → draw more current → heat more: the thermal-electrical feedback that creates current crowding and utilization gradients is resolved, not prescribed.

8COUPLED

Conservation & validation

∫ Qgen dt = ΔUstored + ∫ Qremoved dt     (closure ~ 10−12) (13)
  • Energy: generated = stored + removed to machine precision, every run.
  • Charge: Σik = Iapplied for both collector models, any stack axis.
  • Electrical ledger: I(Uocv − Vterm) = ECM + foil + tab heat, verified to ~10⁻¹².
  • Invariance: rotating the stack axis (x/y/z) leaves results identical to ~10⁻⁷.
  • Analytic limits: 1-D conduction, lumped-capacitance transient, adiabatic ΔT = Q/(mcp), fin closed forms.

No physical constant or coefficient is hard-coded: geometry, materials, tables and boundary conditions all come from configuration — the same equations run any cell you describe.
